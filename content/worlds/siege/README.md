# The Last Ration / Ostatnia racja

A besieged castle. The watch is unpaid, food is finite, and the king hoards grain.
A signed pantry order exposes a departure that abandons soldiers and servants.
No external attackers or scripted breach timer enter the simulation.

The exhausted jailer starts at hunger 70, thirst 55 and fatigue 80 with reduced
combat skill and a weaker sword. Four pantry portions, four raw royal portions,
five cups of kitchen water, one prepared supper and one poison make every trade
material. The male captain suspects theft but has not seen the order.

Escape can follow a real ration/payment bargain, key theft or nonlethal subdual
followed by stealing the guard's key. Regicide can follow convincing the captain
with actual evidence and a deliberate commitment, preparing a direct attack, or
poisoning an actual meal that is delivered and consumed. These are opportunities,
not scripted winning routes. Royal grain is not edible until cooked.

Conrad/Konrad, Nicholas/Mikołaj.
