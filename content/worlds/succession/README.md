# The False Oath / Fałszywa przysięga

An aging king prepares a disputed succession oath. A servant retained the original
memorandum she was told to burn; the physician's own observations contradict the
public account. The captain begins loyal. He knows neither secret.

The king starts in the great hall with reduced combat skill. A bent pin in the
dungeon supports lockpicking. The original memorandum lies beneath a spare livery
in the servants' quarters. One prepared supper, one poison and finite medicines
allow an actual delivery route, not automatic poisoning by dialogue.

Escape can follow a bargain, key theft or the pin, whose failed attempt is noisy.
Evidence can motivate a deliberate alliance, while a prepared direct attack or
actual poisoned-food ingestion can kill the king. A rival succession claim has
no special engine authority, heir actor or scripted victory rule.

Names in English/Polish: Peter/Piotr, Henry/Henryk, Andrew/Andrzej,
Bernard/Bernard, Catherine/Katarzyna, Anne/Anna, Margaret/Małgorzata,
Simon/Szymon, Felix/Feliks, Thomas/Tomasz.
